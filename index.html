01 — About

How I work

Most of my work lives where a product meets an organization: records, permissions, queues, and the screens people use every day. Those constraints are the job. A vague design shows up later, usually in production.

I work across the stack. Angular, TypeScript, and RxJS on the front. Java, Spring Boot, and PostgreSQL on the back, with JUnit and Mockito where behavior has to stay fixed. Delivery includes GitLab CI, Docker, and SonarQube. I have also built content-management flows on Alfresco.

I like shipping clean, scalable code. Small changes, explicit contracts, and writing for the next person who opens the file.
